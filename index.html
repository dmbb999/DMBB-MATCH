Host & MC Guide

Using Your Own Pictures

  1. Place your photos in the images folder

    Put your .jpg, .png, or .webp pictures inside the images/ folder right next to index.html.

  2. Update the picture list in script.js

    Open script.js in any text editor and edit PICTURE_FILES at the very top so the filenames match your photos: 

    const PICTURE_FILES = [
  "family-tree.jpg",
  "santa-hat.jpg",
  "christmas-cookies.png",
  "reindeer.jpg"
];
  3. Add or remove pairs anytime

    Add more filenames to the list to allow more pairs, or remove filenames to shorten the list. Each picture is duplicated automatically to form a matching pair.
