Place your own Christmas party pictures inside this "images" folder!

Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp, .gif

Default expected filenames (customizable in script.js):
- photo1.jpg
- photo2.jpg
- photo3.jpg
- photo4.jpg
- photo5.jpg
- photo6.jpg
- photo7.jpg
- photo8.jpg
- photo9.jpg
- photo10.jpg
- photo11.jpg
- photo12.jpg

To use your own filenames or add/remove pairs:
1. Drop your image files into this "images" folder.
2. Open "script.js" in any text editor.
3. Update the PICTURE_FILES list at the top of script.js to match your exact filenames.
4. Save and open index.html in any web browser (works completely offline).
